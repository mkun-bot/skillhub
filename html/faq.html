Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enroll in a course?

Click on the "Enroll Now" button under your desired course. You will be redirected to the payment page.

What payment methods are supported?

We support PayPal, Credit/Debit Cards, and local options like JazzCash/EasyPaisa.

Can I access courses after payment?

Yes, once payment is confirmed, you will get instant access to your selected course.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes, after completing a course, you will receive a SkillHub certificate.

How can I contact support?

You can use the "Email Support" link or the contact form on the Help page.
